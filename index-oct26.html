Overview

Cover and contents page. States "Revised: OCTOBER, 2026".

Compliance responsibility

  • It is the responsibility of the mounter to ensure the final product complies with state legislation applicable to the state the truck will be registered in.
  • Final approval by an authorised officer and attachment of the resultant modification compliance plate (refer VSB6) (where required) is the mounter's responsibility.

Handover

  • At handover to the customer (or representative) a Cesco Delivery Advise (form EPB0135) must be completed and signed by both Cesco and customer representatives.
    • White copy: placed in the plastic folder for the customer's reference.
    • Yellow copy: returned to Cesco Brisbane, attention Warranty section.
    • Blue copy: retained in the book.
  • Delivery date is to be stamped or etched into the mixer specification plate.

Truck Mounting

Instructions.

Revised: OCTOBER, 2026

NOTE :- It is the responsibility of the mounter to ensure that the final product complies with state legislation applicable to the state the truck will be registered. Final approval by an authorised officer and the attachment of the resultant modification compliance plate (Refer to VSB6) (where required) is the mounters responsibility.

At handover to customer (or their representative) a Cesco Delivery Advise (form EPB0135) must be completed & signed by both the Cesco & customer representatives. The white copy is placed in the plastic folder for the customers reference, the yellow copy is returned to Cesco Brisbane attention Warranty section & the blue copy retained in the book.

Delivery date is to be stamped or etched into the mixer specification plate.
