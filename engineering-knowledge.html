Engineering knowledge

The figures and drawings a mounting has to satisfy before it is signed off.

Drawings, axle capacity, and mixer CG are their own notes. The other volumes are outlined here.

Stability

Roll and grade stability of the finished vehicle. It depends on track, center-of-gravity height, and the load case — empty, water only, and full. Worked examples are not on this page yet. Start from Mixer CG and Axle capacity rather than a similar drum size.

Frame stress

Where the mixer mounts load the chassis rails, and which reinforcements the mounting allows. Stress plots stay with the job file until they are summarised here. Mount positions are the ones in Body mounts.

Hydraulic schemes

Drum-drive circuit: pump, valve, motor, cooler, and reservoir, plus any auxiliary function on the same PTO. A scheme diagram will replace this outline.

Mass properties

Masses used in an approval: bare mixer, water, payload, and the resulting axle split. Keep each figure with the drawing revision it belongs to. Weigh the truck before treating a table as current.

Standards

Vehicle and machinery standards the mounting is checked against. Citations will be added with the clause and the edition, not paraphrased ahead of the register.

Clearances

Cab, exhaust, tyres, suspension travel, PTO, and service points. A clearance that exists on the general arrangement and not on the truck is a miss. The shop checks will be listed here; the sheets themselves live under Drawings.
