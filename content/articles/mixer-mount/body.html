Section 1 — Mounts using U-bolts

  • Use when mounting a mixer to a truck with a flywheel power take-off where hold-down brackets or U-bolts have been supplied. If hold-down brackets supplied → Section 2; frameless mixer → Section 3.
  • A shipping spacer may be fitted between the mixer RHS and the pump mount channel — remove it.
  • Place mixer in position nominated on the Layout Drawing; trial fit driveshaft.
  • Pump mount channel may be repositioned, or mixer moved up to 25 mm from the Layout Drawing position to fit the driveshaft. More than 25 mm → contact Cesco Engineering Department, Brisbane, IMMEDIATELY.
  • Fixing pump mount channel to mixer RHS: see Chapter 4 (driveline).

Source text “Ensure U-bolts and Fishplates can be positioned as detailed in this Book.”

  • Any problems fitting driveshaft, U-bolts or fishplates → contact Cesco Brisbane immediately.
  • All U-bolts must have a spacer inside the truck chassis (trimmed to tap fit) so the chassis is not collapsed; where air/brake lines crowd the chassis, improvise (e.g. small-diameter pipe with retainer over the U-bolt leg).

Fishplates — chassis drilling rules

  • Use existing chassis holes where possible (within limits of Mixer Position drawing).
  • Holes drilled and deburred both sides.
  • No hole > 17 mm diameter; hole ≤ 1 mm larger than bolt diameter.
  • Holes not closer than 50 mm from top or bottom flange.
  • New holes ≥ 50 mm between centres or from existing holes/bolts/rivets.
  • Cross-member flange/web holes: > 25 mm from hole edge to nearest material edge.
  • No more than two holes in a vertical line. Never drill in top or bottom flange.
  • Fasteners: high tensile Grade 10.9 (minimum Grade 4.6). Existing chassis bolts: at least one full thread through the nut after tightening; if too short replace with same diameter and Grade.
  • Packer plates (not supplied — mounting agent's responsibility) may be used between fishplate and truck or mixer chassis; thickness selected to avoid stress on fishplate/chassis.

Chassis packers

  • Between truck chassis and splash tray; required for certain trucks and in some states; supplied only when required.
  • Nylon; drill to clear rivet heads if needed.
  • Supplied as three pieces 100 x 10 x 3000 mm; 1.5 lengths each side to cover nominal 4500 mm mixer chassis; dovetail the join; cut with hacksaw.

Splash tray

  • Poly splash tray fitted as 1 length, fully supporting mixer RHS full length; fit under aluminium end plate P/N 501637; fix with at least 7 stainless M6 x 25 mm screws and nuts.
  • Drain holes: 1 per 6.5 metre & 2 per 7.5 metre; hole saw 89 mm dia; fit gutter pipe P/N 503154 (shown in photo). Position so run-off does not fall on hydraulic pump, drive shaft, actuator etc.
  • Limited hosing may pass through drain holes if advantageous.
  • U-bolt holes: holesaw neat fit, silicone sealant after tightening U-bolts.
  • Notch outer edge where needed to clear U-bolts, side restraints or upper mount angles.
  • Fit aluminium angle P/N 503155 in front of the drain holes to prevent run-off over the front edge.

Fitting nylon chassis packer & splash tray (recommended)

  1. Thick bead of contact cement along top flange of truck chassis.
  2. Place plastic chassis packers.
  3. Thick bead of contact cement along top of packer.
  4. Position splash trays over packers.

Ensures packers and trays don't move while positioning the mixer.

Section 2 — Mounts using hold-down brackets

  • For FWTO trucks with hold-down brackets supplied. Remove shipping spacer if fitted.
  • Upper & lower mounting angles and bolts supplied. Holes for mounting angles (where existing holes can't be used) 16.5 mm dia; drill bit in good condition.
  • Drawing 509934 shows correct bracket positioning (referenced as "attached"; not embedded in supplied file).
  • Suggested: fix all lower mounting angles (some with round bar on outer edge) to truck chassis before fitting mixer, using huck bolts or grade 8.8 M16 (minimum grade) bolts; slight trimming may be needed to clear truck fixtures.
  • No welding to truck chassis.
  • Place mixer per Layout Drawing; trial fit driveshaft; same 25 mm rule as Section 1.
  • G-clamp frame to truck chassis; place upper mount angles against side of mixer frame, square, resting on top of the steel packer to the lower angle. A spacer may be needed between upper angle and mixer frame if the lower angle sits over a chassis reinforcing plate.
  • Upper mount angles: tack weld, then final weld using vertical-up welds; weld 3 sides only (two sides and top); remove paint around angles first. (Welding here is to the mixer frame.)
  • Spot-drill 16.5 mm holes through lower angle; bolt with M16 class 10.9 x 60 long bolts, structural high-tensile washers and Cone Lok nuts (single use only — do not reuse).
  • Tension to 300 Nm.

Source text “Tension to 300 Newton metres, using accepted industry standards.”

Section 3 — Frameless mixer mount (chassis angles on trucks)

Rear pedestal position

  • Rear end assembly (rear pedestal, A-frame, discharge assembly) positioned by a given measurement from the centre line of the truck rear axle group to the rear face of the rear pedestal foot plates (point "A" on drawings). Dimension calculated by Cesco Engineering and supplied to the workshop before mounting.
  • Mark rear edge of foot plate on the rear chassis angle.

Front pedestal position

  • Mark rear-most position of front pedestal on front chassis angles at the distance from rear pedestal shown on the relevant drawing (see Appendix A; ask Engineering for the drawing for the mixer specification). Pedestal square to truck centreline.
  • Lift rear end assembly onto chassis angles up to the mark; centre on truck centreline, square, foot plates resting on chassis angles; clamp.

Source text “Using suitable lifting gear, place the rear end assembly at the rear of the truck, on the chassis angles provided, up to the mark made previously.”

  • Lift front (gearbox) pedestal onto front chassis angles at marked distance; square; clamp.

Source text “Using suitable lifting gear, place the front (gear box ) pedestal on the front chassis angles at the distance from the rear pedestal as marked of the front chassis angles. Ensure that the pedestal is square to the truck centreline. Clamp pedestal in position.”

  • Assemble trunnion rollers to rear pedestal and gearbox to front pedestal; position barrel on trunnion rollers and mount to gearbox.
  • Check track ring on trunnion rollers for squareness and approx 10–15 mm between rear face of trunnion rollers and rear face of track ring; adjust rear pedestal if needed.
  • Check rear edge of gearbox pedestal base plate does not overhang chamfer on chassis angle; if small overhang <20 mm, move rear pedestal rearwards by the same amount to keep pedestal spacing.
  • Remove barrel; mark chassis angles with hole positions matching pedestal foot-plate holes.

Mixer assembly

  • Remove rear end assembly and gearbox pedestal; drill marked holes in chassis angles both sides.
  • Refit pedestals and bolt with Grade 10.9 bolts, structural washers and cone-lock nuts; tighten to 300 Nm (221 ft.lbs) and mark bolt head.
  • Finish assembling mixer per specified assembly details.
  • Chassis packers & splash trays: as Section 1.

Mixer Mounting

FWTO Truck Mounting Procedures

Section 1 – Mounts using U-bolts.

Use this Procedure when Truck Mounting a mixer to a truck with a flywheel power take-off, & hold-down brackets or U-bolts have been supplied.

A spacer may have been fitted between the mixer RHS & the pump mount channel for shipping purposes. If so, remove this spacer.

Note that certain truck suppliers will not warrant the truck chassis if U-bolts are used to secure a body.

If hold-down Brackets have been supplied, refer to Section 2.

If a frameless mixer is to be mounted, refer to Section 3.

Place Mixer onto Truck in position nominated on Layout Drawing.

Trial Fit Driveshaft.

Pump mount channel may be repositioned on mixer chassis, or mixer may be moved up to 25mm from the position nominated on the Layout Drawing to fit Driveshaft correctly. If Mixer needs to be moved more than 25mm contact Cesco Engineering Department in Brisbane - IMMEDIATELY.

For details of fixing pump mount channel to mixer RHS refer Chapter 4.

Ensure U-bolts and Fishplates can be positioned as detailed in this Book.

If there are any problems with fitting Driveshaft, U-bolts or Fishplates contact Cesco in Brisbane - IMMEDIATELY.

All U-bolts must be fitted with a spacer inside the truck chassis, to ensure the truck chassis is not collapsed by tightening the U-bolt. Spacer length must be trimmed to be a tap fit into truck chassis. Where truck has hardware (eg air or brake lines) crowding the inside of the chassis, improvisation may be necessary, i.e. a length of small diameter pipe with a retainer to slip over the U-bolt leg.

Fishplates

Where possible, (within the limitations of the Mixer Position drawing) position Fishplates to utilise existing truck chassis holes. Where this is not possible, additional holes must be drilled in the truck chassis with the following restrictions:

All holes must be drilled and deburred both sides.

No holes allowed to exceed 17mm diameter.

Hole diameter must be no more than 1mm greater than bolt diameter.

Hole diameter must not be closer than 50mm from top or bottom flange.

New holes must not be less than 50mm between centres or from existing holes, bolts or rivets.

If for any reason there is a need to drill holes into a cross-member flange or web, it must measure more than 25mm from the edge of the hole to the nearest edge of the material.

Drill no more than two (2) holes in a vertical line.

NEVER DRILL HOLES IN TOP OR BOTTOM FLANGE.

All fasteners should be high tensile Grade 10.9 (minimum Grade 4.6). Where existing chassis bolts are used, bolt length must be sufficient to ensure that at least one full thread projects through the nut after tightening. If bolts are too short, they must be replaced with a bolt of the same diameter and of the same Grade.

To allow for variations in truck chassis width, packer plates may be used with the Fishplates. Packer plates will not be supplied with the truck mounting kit - they are the responsibility of the truck mounting agent. They may be fitted between the Fishplate and the truck or Mixer chassis. In each case the thickness of the packer plates must be carefully selected to ensure that no unnecessary stress is placed on the Fishplate, truck chassis or Mixer chassis.

UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES MAY ANYTHING BE WELDED TO THE TRUCK CHASSIS.

The following drawings specify the correct location of ‘U’ bolts and Fishplates.

509051 For Mixers with 100x100 RHS or 102x76 RHS on Truck Chassis narrower than 860mm.

509052 For Mixers with 100x100 RHS or 102x76 RHS on Truck Chassis 860mm.

509053 For Mixers with 100x100 RHS or 102x76 RHS on Truck Chassis wider than 860mm.

Chassis Packers

Chassis packers, which are placed between the truck chassis and splash tray, are required for certain trucks and in some states. They will be supplied only when required.

When chassis packers are supplied, they will be Nylon. If required, the packers should be drilled as to clear any rivet heads in the top of the truck chassis.

Chassis packers are supplied as three pieces 100 x 10 x 3000mm long. One and a half lengths are required each side to cover the nominal 4500mm long Mixer chassis. Dovetail the join. These packers can be readily cut with a hack saw.

Splash Tray

The poly splash tray should be fitted as 1 length, fully supporting the mixer RHS for its entire length. It should be installed to fit under the aluminium end plate (Part No. 501637) and fixed with at least 7 stainless M6 x 25mm screws and nuts.

Drain holes (1 per 6.5 metre & 2 per 7.5 metre) are to be installed. Using a hole saw, drill 89mm diameter, then fit the gutter pipe (P/N 503154) as shown in photo. It is important that these drain holes are positioned so that run off does not fall onto such items as, hydraulic pump, drive shaft, actuator etc.

Fitting of Nylon Chassis Packer & Splash Tray

Recommended installation as follows:

Run a thick bead of Contact Cement along the top flange of the truck chassis.

Place the plastic chassis packers in the required position.

Run a thick bead of Contact Cement along the top of the chassis packer.

Position the splash trays over the top of the chassis packers.

This procedure ensures that the chassis packers and splash trays do not move during positioning of the Mixer on the Truck chassis.

Section 2 Mounts using Hold-down Brackets.

Use this Procedure when Truck Mounting a Mixer to a Truck with a flywheel power take off & hold-down brackets have been supplied.

A spacer may have been fitted between the mixer RHS & the pump mount channel for shipping purposes. If so, remove this spacer.

Upper & lower mounting angles (together with mounting bolts) will have been supplied with the kit. All holes drilled for mounting angles (where an existing hole cannot be utilised) must be 16.5 mm dia. Drill bit must be in good condition.

Drawing 509934 (attached) shows correct positioning of brackets.

It is suggested that all lower mounting angles, (in some cases, the ones with a length of round bar along outer edge), are fixed to the truck chassis prior to fitting the mixer. Either huck bolts or grade 8.8 M16 (minimum grade) bolts may be used. In some cases, slight trimming of this lower angle may be necessary to clear existing fixtures of the truck.

UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES MAY ANYTHING BE WELDED TO THE TRUCK CHASSIS.

Place Mixer onto Truck in position nominated on Layout Drawing.

Trial Fit Driveshaft.

Pump mount channel may be repositioned on mixer chassis, or mixer may be moved up to 25mm from the position nominated on the Layout Drawing to fit Driveshaft correctly. If Mixer needs to be moved more than 25mm - contact Cesco in Brisbane - IMMEDIATELY.

For details of fixing pump mount channel to mixer RHS refer Chapter 4.

Once the mixer is in position, G clamp the frame to the truck chassis so the upper mount angles can be positioned. Place the upper mount angles in their finished position against the side of the mixer frame, square to the mixer frame, & resting on the top of the steel packer to the lower mount angle.

In some cases, a spacer may be required between the upper mount angle & the mixer frame, if the lower mount angle has been fitted over a reinforcing plate on the truck chassis.

It is suggested that the upper mount angles be tack welded first, then final welded using vertical up welds for maximum strength. Weld 3 sides only, the two sides and the top. All paint around the angles should be removed to ensure a weld with maximum structural integrity.

The 16.5mm dia. holes can now be spot drilled through the lower mount angle, & bolted using the M16 class 10.9 x 60 long bolts, structural high-tensile washers & Cone Lok nuts supplied. NOTE that the Cone Lok nuts are single use only, & must not be reused.

Tension to 300 Newton metres, using accepted industry standards.
