Selection of drive shaft (1350 vs 1410)

  1. Length: do NOT use 1350 when driveshaft working length > 1000 mm and pump capacity > 71cc. All measurements refer to working length.
  2. Universal joint type: 1350 — clamp yoke and flange type combo on the two ends; 1410 — flange-to-flange universal joints both ends.
  3. Joint angles: once installed, both 1350 and 1410 must have joint angles between 3–5 degrees, and equal to each other.
  4. Foam-filled: working length over 1000 mm must be foam-filled type (reduce harmonic resonance).
  5. Teeth: 71cc pump — 21T splined couplings — 1350 or 1410 (subject to length limit). 90cc pump — 23T splined couplings — 1410 only.
  6. Greaseable or non-greaseable U-joints: confirm from the spec sheet.
  7. Additional: check bolt pattern provided with the shaft selection form before ordering. Maintenance warning: do NOT use high-pressure acid/water wash-down on 1350 or 1410 driveshafts.

Measuring drive shaft & pump mount angles

  1. Use a flat section of the vehicle chassis as datum for calibrating the level.
  2. PTO output flange and pump input flange parallel (max variation 1 degree).
  3. Centre point of PTO flange vs centre point of pump input flange not off by more than 6 degrees compared to the other.
  4. Limits apply in both vertical and horizontal planes.

Truck exhaust — WARNING

If any installation requires removing the exhaust pipe, direct the muffler outlet towards the ground away from truck & mixer components before starting the engine. Hot exhaust can damage hoses, paint etc. and could cause braking system failure if driven. Where impossible, take suitable precautions to prevent heat damage before driving.

Driveshaft & pump mount channel installation

  • Pump mount channel engineered to achieve correct alignment angles from truck info supplied to Cesco Australia Ltd. If in doubt or angles look excessive, contact Cesco immediately; max allowable angle info from Cesco Engineering Department.
  • After positioning mixer, trial fit driveshaft; channel may be slightly repositioned. Always trial fit before attaching mixer to truck and before fixing pump mount to mixer chassis.
  • Preferred attachment of pump mount channel to mixer chassis: welding; if impractical, nutserts.

Drive shaft guard

  • Supplied guard may need modification by mounter; must conform to acceptable industry guarding standards.
  • Where guard is closer than 130 mm (finger reach) to a danger point, secondary modification (e.g. rubber sheeting) may be necessary.
  • Mounter to attach the supplied 'Caution' sticker adjacent to the guard.

Drive Line.

Selection of Drive Shaft

The following criteria should be adopted when choosing between a 1350 and a 1410 driveshaft.

  1. Driveshaft Length:
  • Do NOT use 1350 Driveshaft when driveshaft length is greater than 1000mm and pump capacity is higher than 71cc.
  • All measurements refer to the working length of the driveshaft.
  1. Universal Joint Type:
  • 1350 Driveshaft: Use clamp yoke and flange type combo on the two ends.
  • 1410 Driveshaft: Use flange-to-flange universal joints on both ends.
  1. Universal Joint Angle Requirements:
  • Once installed, both 1350 and 1410 driveshafts must have their joint angles between 3–5 degrees and their angles must be equal to each other.
  1. Foam-Filled Requirements:
  • Driveshafts with a working length over 1000mm must be foam-filled type to reduce harmonic resonance.
  1. Number of Teeth:
  • 71cc pump - 21T splined couplings – Both 1350 and 1410 can be used – subjected to working length limitation
  • 90cc pump - 23T splined couplings – Only 1410 drive shafts should be used.
  1. Greaseable or Non-Greaseable Universal Joints:
  • Confirm whether universal joints are greaseable or non-greaseable based on the spec sheet.
  1. Additional Guidelines:
  • Check the bolt pattern provided with the shaft selection form and confirm before ordering.
  • Maintenance Warning: Do NOT use high-pressure acid / water wash-down on either 1350 or 1410 driveshafts.

Measuring Drive Shaft & Pump Mount Angles

Please adhere to following guidelines when measuring the driveshaft angles for pump mount installation / fabrication.

  1. A flat section of the vehicle chassis shall be used as the datum for calibrating the level.
  2. The PTO output flange and the input flange of the pump should be parallel to each other (or a maximum variation of 1 degree)
  3. The centre point of the PTO flange and the centre point of the Pump input flange should not be off by more than 6 degrees compared to the other.
  4. These maximum permissible angels are applicable to both vertical and horizontal planes.

Truck Exhaust

WARNING

If installation of any component necessitates the removal of the exhaust pipe, the outlet of the muffler must be directed towards the ground, away from all truck & mixer components, before starting the truck engine. High temperatures of exhaust gases will cause damage to hoses, paint etc & could cause failure of the braking system if truck is driven. In circumstances where it is impossible to direct the muffler outlet away from all possible danger points, suitable precautions must be taken to prevent any heat damage before the truck is driven.

Driveshaft & Pump Mount Channel Installation

The pump mount channel supplied with the mixer has been engineered to position the pump to achieve the correct driveshaft alignment angles, based on the truck information supplied to Cesco Australia Ltd. If the mounter has any doubts as to correct alignment, or the angles appear excessive, they should contact Cesco immediately to clarify the situation. Extra information regarding maximum allowable angles, is available from Cesco Engineering Department.

After positioning the mixer onto the truck chassis, trial fit the driveshaft. The pump mount channel may be slightly repositioned to assist correct driveshaft fitment. Always trial fit the driveshaft before attaching the mixer to the truck & before fixing the pump mount to the mixer chassis.

The preferred method of attaching the pump mount channel to the mixer chassis is by welding. If this is impractical, nutserts may be used.

Drive Shaft Guard

The driveshaft guard supplied with the mixer may require modification by the mounter to fit correctly. The final installation must conform to acceptable industry standards for guarding. Where the guard is closer than 130mm (finger reach) to a danger point, secondary modification to the supplied guard (eg, fitting of rubber sheeting), to prevent finger entry, may be necessary.

The ‘Caution’ sticker supplied is to be attached adjacent to the guard by the mounter.
