Unpacking.

Shipping List

Mixer Mounting Kits should be checked against the Shipping List within 48 hours of receipt, and the Delivery Confirmation Advice (with a list of any problems), returned to Cesco in Brisbane. (email: info@cescoaustralia.com, attention of Stores Supervisor) This will eliminate any delay in mounting due to parts being found to have been short supplied halfway through the job.

Paint Touch-up

Should the mixer require any touch-up paint, to repair scratches and other minor paint damage incurred during transport and truck mounting please email info@cescoaustralia.com., who will advise the colour specification and the nearest supplier to you.

If excessive paint damage has occurred, spray painting will be required. Contract Cesco Australia or their distributor.
