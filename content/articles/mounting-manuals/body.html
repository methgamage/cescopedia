Drive Line (Chapter 4) — driveshaft selection, angles, pump mount, guard

Chapter 4 — New 1350 vs 1410 driveshaft selection rules (length >1000 mm & pump >71cc → not 1350; 90cc/23T → 1410 only; foam-filled >1000 mm; joint angles 3–5° and equal); measuring rules for PTO/pump flange angles (parallel ±1°, 90±6°); exhaust warning; pump mount trial-fit and welding preference; guard 130 mm finger-reach rule; Caution sticker. See Drive Line.

Water System (Chapter 5) — tank, hold-back valve, regulator

Chapter 5 — Tank supplied mounted or loose, fittings loose; hold-back valve per VSB6 on auxiliary tank, port 2 in / port 3 out, opens 620 kPa (90 psi) closes 607 kPa (88 psi); J-brackets bolted never welded; drilling rules (≤0.5 mm oversize, ≥60 mm from flange); regulator approx 35 psi, ≤80% of tank design pressure (310 kPa / 45 psi). See Water System. 1 source flag

Initial Start Up Procedure — 90 Series (Chapter 9)

Chapter 9 — Loosen bounce strap per Operators Manual; bypass pause valve with ¼" ball valve; 10-micron filtered fluid; fill inlet line and pump/motor housings; start in neutral, idle, ramp; cycle chute jack; close ball valve; cycle mix/discharge ≥5 min; refit pause valve and retest. Two WARNING boxes (neutral swashplate, vehicle disabling). See Initial Start Up — 90 Series. 1 source flag
