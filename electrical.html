Remote Controls.

Installation instructions are included in the Operators Manual included with each mixer.

For truck specific instructions, contact Cesco Australia Ltd, Engineering Dept. Brisbane, phone 07 3908 6088

Important Points

For automatic transmission trucks, always check that the throttle cannot be operated from the hand piece unless transmission is in park/neutral mode. The mixer must not be released unless this function is working correctly.

If complete system does not operate, always check emergency stop button has not been activated.

Do not overtighten the pause valve mounting bolts, as excessive tightening may cause a clamping effect which could prevent the valve from operating. If the valve does not operate, slightly loosen the mounting bolts & test again.

Before final connection of the pause valve, refer Chapter 14, Initial Start Up Procedure for special instructions.
