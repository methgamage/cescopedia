Embedded drawing 500371 (image — read visually, verify)

  • Title block: CESCO AUSTRALIA LIMITED — "HYDRAULICS HOSES AND FITTINGS F.B.U. JIC FITTINGS KIT EX HCM"; drawn MK 19/6/14; Rev A "HOSE LENGTHS & FITTINGS CHANGED" (MK, 3/15). Note: a copy of this drawing to be included in every kit.
  • Panels: suction lines (500370 suction filter plus gauge; black synthetic rubber suction hose 1" NB x 950 short or 1500 long; 505388 plastic guard where reqd), high pressure lines (1" bore multi-spiral, M39x2 24° male ends, 950 mm short / 1450 mm long; 505397 (SG155) spring guard where reqd), cooling lines and case drain line (3/4" push-on multi-purpose hose, 505388 (SG122) guard; lengths shown 1250/1800 and 1200/1500 mm short/long), pause valve on case drain.
  • Parts list items 1–13 (adaptors, elbows, coupling, T-bolt clamp, 2 mm orifice adaptor) as tabulated on the drawing.

Embedded drawing 500360 (image — read visually, verify)

  • Title: "KIT – CHUTE RAISE & LOWER REXROTH CONTROL VALVE (504790 – 504797) (FITTINGS KIT)"; drawn BJG 02/07/2008; Rev D.
  • Shows pump (port Ps), control valve ports T/B/A/P, all-in-one cooler, cylinder; 3/8" hoses with 9/16" JIC fittings (13-item parts list).
  • Note on drawing: If frame has no ASL ladder, mount control valve to the gearbox pedestal with bracket 516814 in position shown in drawing SK-4907. Include a copy of this drawing with every kit.
  • Revision history: A ECO No. M0620 new part (BJG 02/07/2008); B ECO No. M0640 new oil reservoir, filter and fittings (EDB 09/12/2009); C ECO No. M0747 changed hose lengths and fittings for control valve position (MK 21/10/15); D ECO No. M1042 item no 13 matched the actual kit & all-in-one cooler added (MG 01/10/2025).
