Mudguards.
A range of options for mudguard types exists.
Steel (or aluminium) fabricated mudguards (traditional running board type) will normally be fitted to the mixer before shipment.
2 x 16mm grommets are supplied. These are to be fitted where the taillight wiring passes through the guard, to prevent chafing. If wire is not tight in grommet, fill gap with silicone to exclude water etc.
Plastic Mudguards are mounted to the truck chassis using offset pole brackets. Existing chassis holes can usually be utilised, as the offset bracket allows flexibility. The mudguard must be positioned evenly over the tyre, & with a clearance of at least 160mm from the top of the tyre to the guard, at the vertical centreline. The flange should be just outside the outer edge of the tyre.
Mudflaps should be bent to achieve a minimum clearance of 50mm from the tyre.
Rolled Aluminium Mudguards are mounted as per Plastic guards.
